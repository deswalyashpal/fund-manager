Labels are set by the monthly test and drop automatically if a record slips.

When would the Sensex call say sell?

Only when the Sensex closes below the exit line (a trailing line that rises with the trend). Intraday dips don't count. Buy back on a close above it.

How should I add new money?

Normally, invest as planned. When the market is stretched (heat/RSI above 70), split new money into 4 equal parts, one each week. Avoid lump sums within 2 days of RBI, Fed or CPI dates. On dips of 10% / 20% from the 1-year high, add extra instalments.

Why is the pool holding cash?

Any market below its 100-day average (checked at the Friday close, in rupees) is held at half weight; the rest waits in a liquid fund. Tested since 2008, this roughly halved the worst fall for a small cost in return.

When will the gold & silver call say buy?

When the price closes above its buy-signal line and at least 4 of 10 trend checks are positive. Buy in stages. While the gold/silver ratio is above 85, favour silver.

Words used on this site
Trend score
Out of 10 simple checks, how many say "up".
100-day average
Average close of the last 100 trading days (about 5 months).
Heat (RSI)
0–100 gauge of how fast prices moved. Above 70 = ran up fast.
Exit / buy-signal line
A trailing line (Supertrend). Close below it in an uptrend = exit; above it in a downtrend = buy.
Range
Where the price should stay 8 times in 10.
Liquid fund
A very low-risk debt fund used to park cash.
IBJA rate
India's benchmark gold/silver rate, before GST and making charges.
Live
A price from a market still trading; not a close yet.
3 pm cut-off
Index-fund orders before 3:00 pm IST get that day's closing price.
How often does this page update?

On trading days at 08:45 and 09:10 (before the open), every hour from 10:20 to 15:20, at 14:40 (before the 3 pm cut-off), 16:15 (after the close), 23:30 and 03:00 IST, plus Saturday checks. Any open page reloads itself when a new update is published.

Where does the data come from?

Prices: Yahoo Finance. Indian gold/silver: IBJA. US Fed range: FRED. News: Economic Times, Mint, Business Standard, CNBC, MarketWatch and Google News feeds, picked by fixed rules (no AI). Research to inform decisions, not investment advice.
