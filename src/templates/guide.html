Labels are set automatically from the monthly validation run. A forecast drops to amber or red if its record slips.

Plain-word guide

Trend score
Out of 10 simple checks (price above its averages, momentum up, and so on), how many say "up".
100-day average
The average closing price of the last 100 trading days (about 5 months). Above it = healthy trend.
RSI
A 0–100 gauge of how fast prices moved recently. Above 70 = ran up fast (often pauses); below 30 = fell fast.
Supertrend line / exit line
A trailing stop line. A close below it in an uptrend is the exit signal; a close above it in a downtrend is the buy signal.
Range (80%)
The band the price should stay inside 8 times out of 10.
Stagger
Investing new money in 4 equal parts, one each week, instead of all at once.
Risk parity
Giving calmer markets more money and jumpy markets less, so each adds similar risk.
Liquid fund
A very low-risk debt fund used as a parking place for cash.
Gold/silver ratio
How many ounces of silver one ounce of gold buys. High = silver is cheap relative to gold.
Live
A price from a market that is still trading. It is not a closing price yet.
Model out of step
Shown when the day's-close forecast got the direction wrong on more than 6 of the last 10 days (below 40% over 20 days). Forecasts are then down-weighted.
COMEX futures
Gold and silver contracts traded in New York. Used for all metal signals here; spot prices in the news can differ slightly.
3 pm cut-off
Index-fund orders placed before 3:00 pm IST get that day's closing price (NAV). The Sensex closes at 3:30 pm.

The rules this site follows

  1. Sensex fund: hold by default. Sell only on a daily close below the exit line. When stretched (RSI above 70), new money goes in 4 weekly parts. No lump sums in the 2 days around RBI/Fed/CPI dates.
  2. 2:15 pm check: if the Sensex is more than 0.5% below yesterday's close and an instalment is due, place it before 3 pm. Never sell on this signal.
  3. Dip ladder: extra new money at 10% below the 1-yr high, more at 20%, the most if India VIX passes 25.
  4. Holding rule: money needed within 1 year stays in a liquid fund; money for 3+ years goes in the index fund.
  5. Global pool: risk-parity weights reset monthly; any market below its 100-day average (checked at the Friday close) is held at half weight.
  6. Gold and silver: no buying while the trend score is 3 or less. Buy signal = close above the Supertrend line with a trend score of 4+. Favour silver while the ratio is above 85.
