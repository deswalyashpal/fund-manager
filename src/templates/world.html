What it means for decisions

  • India rarely crashed straight after these splits: the odds of a 5%+ drop were about half the normal level, so there is no reason to sell.
  • But gains were muted for about 3 months. India usually stops running ahead rather than the world catching up quickly.
  • So: hold, don't chase, and stagger new money.

Clock-aligned: at each Indian close only markets that had already closed count (Asia same day; Europe and US previous session). This differs slightly from the research version, which used same-date values. Sample sizes are small; treat as a tilt.
